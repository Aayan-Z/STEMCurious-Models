Refraction
Ray bends toward the normal
Light is entering a medium with a higher refractive index, so its speed decreases and the refracted ray bends toward the normal.
Angles are measured from the normal, not from the surface. The reflected ray is always shown because a real boundary generally reflects some light as well as transmitting it.
