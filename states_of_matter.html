Interpretation

Heating changes the particles gradually. In a solid, neighbors vibrate around fixed positions. As bonds weaken, the lattice loosens into a liquid. With further heating, particles escape the liquid and spread through the container as a gas. Gravity acts on all particles, while gas motion is strong enough to fill the available space.
